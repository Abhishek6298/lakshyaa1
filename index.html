PHYSICS • ROTATIONAL MOTION

Mistake Diagnosis & Examiner Trap Warning

Silly Mistake Flagged

🔍 Mistake Root Cause:

"You applied Parallel Axis Theorem incorrectly by taking the axis at distance d from an arbitrary parallel line instead of the center of mass axis (I = Icm + Md2)."

💡 NCERT Principle:

Moment of inertia about any axis parallel to a body through its center of mass is the sum of moment of inertia about center of mass and Md2.

⚡ 10-Second Shortcut:

Never memorize formulas for shifting non-CM axes. Always compute Icm first, then add Md2.

🧪 Reimer-Tiemann vs Kolbe's Reaction:

• Reimer-Tiemann: Phenol + CHCl3 + aq. NaOH ➔ Salicylaldehyde (Electrophile: Dichlorocarbene :CCl2)

• Kolbe's Reaction: Phenol + NaOH + CO2 (398K, 4–7 atm) ➔ Salicylic Acid (Electrophile: CO2)

⚠️ Examiner Negative-Marking Trap (-1 Mark):

Students often mark CCl4 instead of CHCl3. Remember: Phenol + CCl4 + NaOH gives Salicylic Acid, NOT Salicylaldehyde!
